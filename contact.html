Reporting a problem

You will get a faster fix if you include these details:

  • Your browser and device (for example, Chrome on Windows or Safari on iPhone).
  • The type of file and roughly how large it was.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.

Please do not attach private photos. A description, or a similar sample image, is enough.
